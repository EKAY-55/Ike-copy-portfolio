A little about me

Psychology first.
Copy second.

I'm Ike, a SaaS and technology copywriter with a background in accounting.

My approach to copywriting is rooted in understanding people: what they need, what frustrates them, what influences their decisions and what makes them trust a product.

My accounting background has shaped how I think about structure, evidence and analysis. Copywriting gives me another way to apply that thinking: turning complex ideas into messages people can understand and act on.

The best copy doesn't feel like copy. It feels like someone finally understood your problem before offering you a solution.

What I focus on

SaaS copywriting, messaging strategy, landing pages and email sequences.

How I work

Research-led thinking, customer psychology and purposeful writing.
